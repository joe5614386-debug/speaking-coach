    How scoring works: the browser's speech recognition listens, then the text is checked phrase by phrase. Accuracy: a phrase is green once you said it in order in any attempt, so restarting or correcting yourself is fine. Fillers (uh, um), repeated words and “I mean” are ignored. Yellow words were heard only in pieces (half credit); red words were missed. Fluency is scored separately and goes down with restarts, fillers, repeats and a slow pace. You get 15 seconds per sentence (longer for a whole passage); listening stops once every phrase is green, or after a few seconds of silence. It measures whether a listener would understand you, not individual sounds.
